Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 September 2026

Pakka ("we", "our", "the app") is an AI-assisted packing planner. This policy explains what information Pakka collects, how it's used, and the choices you have — written in plain language, not legalese.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, a securely hashed password (we never store or see your actual password), and an optional display name.
  • Trips you create: destination, dates, purpose, and activities you add.
  • Wardrobe and packing list items: names, categories, and quantities of items you add manually or that Pakka detects from a photo.
  • Photos you choose to upload: photos of items you're packing, used to automatically detect what's in them.
  • Basic usage data: a monthly count of how many photos you've had analyzed, used only to apply fair-use limits on the free tier.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, or any advertising identifiers, and we do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

  • To provide the app's core features — your trips, wardrobe, and packing lists.
  • Photos you upload are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to identify the items in them. This is the only way photo detection works; we don't run our own image recognition.
  • The destination name of a trip is sent to Open-Meteo (for weather forecasts) and Wikimedia (for a destination photo) — no account information is included in these requests, just the place name.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising, under any circumstances.

Who we share it with

Pakka relies on a small number of service providers to operate:

  • Anthropic — processes photos and item data you submit, to power item detection. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Railway — hosts our server, database, and uploaded photos.
  • Open-Meteo and Wikimedia — provide weather forecasts and destination photos, given only a place name.

None of these providers are permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app — this immediately and permanently deletes your trips, wardrobe, packing lists, and every uploaded photo. This cannot be undone.

Your rights

You can access and edit most of your data directly in the app. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time (see above). For any other request — such as exporting your data — contact us using the details below.

Security

Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to your data requires an authenticated session tied to your account.

Children's privacy

Pakka is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continuing to use Pakka after a change means you accept the updated policy.
