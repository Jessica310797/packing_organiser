Common questions

How do I delete my account and data?
Open the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, trips, wardrobe, packing lists, and any uploaded photos, and cannot be undone.

Why can't I scan any more photos this month?
Photo scanning (automatic item detection) is limited to a set number per month on the free tier, to keep the service sustainable. The limit resets on the 1st of each month. You can always add items manually with no limit.

Is my data private?
Yes — see our Privacy Policy for exactly what's collected and how it's used.
